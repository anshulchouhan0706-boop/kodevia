What you get

  • One app for iOS and Android, built cross-platform with Flutter or React Native, or fully native when the product needs it. We explain the trade-off before you choose.

Apps we build

Ordering and delivery apps for food, retail and everyday commerce. Booking apps for services, clinics and classes. Customer and loyalty apps that bring people back. Internal apps for field teams, inventory and operations.

Who it is for

Businesses in Indore whose customers come back often enough to keep an app on their phone, and founders who want to launch a product. If you are not sure an app is the right first step, we will tell you honestly; sometimes a fast website does the job sooner and for less.

How a project runs

  1. Discover. We learn the business, the people who will use it and the constraints, and agree what success looks like. You get a scope, a timeline and a fixed quote before you commit.
  2. Design. Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype you can try on your own phone, then the final visual design.
  3. Build. Development in short rounds on a private staging link, so you see real progress instead of status reports.
  4. Launch and care. Hosting, store submission where needed, analytics and search setup, then ongoing support after day one.

Questions

How much does it cost to build an app in Indore?

A focused first version with Kodevia starts from ₹1,00,000. Cost depends mainly on the number of screens, features such as payments or live tracking, and whether you need iOS, Android or both. You get a fixed quote after discovery.

Flutter, React Native or native: which should I choose?

For most business apps, one cross-platform codebase covers iOS and Android at lower cost. Native makes sense for heavy device features or demanding performance. We recommend one after Discover, with the reasons.

Do you publish the app to the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare the listings and submit to both stores, with the developer accounts in your name so you own the app.

Who owns the app and the code?

You do. The code repositories, design files and store accounts are set up in your name from the start.
