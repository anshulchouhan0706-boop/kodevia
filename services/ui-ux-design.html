Why design comes first

Changing a screen in a prototype takes minutes; changing it after it is built takes days. Good UX design catches confusing steps, missing features and wrong assumptions while they are still cheap to fix, and it is usually the difference between a product people tolerate and one they enjoy using.

Who it is for

Founders validating an idea, businesses in Indore planning a new website or app, and teams whose existing product feels confusing, dated or inconsistent. Design can be a standalone project or the first stage of a full build with us.

How a project runs

  1. Discover. We learn the business, the people who will use it and the constraints, and agree what success looks like. You get a scope, a timeline and a fixed quote before you commit.
  2. Design. Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype you can try on your own phone, then the final visual design.
  3. Build. Development in short rounds on a private staging link, so you see real progress instead of status reports.
  4. Launch and care. Hosting, store submission where needed, analytics and search setup, then ongoing support after day one.

Questions

What is the difference between UI and UX design?

UX design is how the product works: the steps, the structure and how easy it is to get things done. UI design is how it looks and feels: layout, type, colour and detail. You need both, and we do both.

Can you design for my existing developers to build?

Yes. We hand over organised Figma files and a design system with every component and state specified, so any competent team can build from it.

Do I need a prototype?

For anything beyond a simple website, yes. A clickable prototype lets you and your customers try the product before the build, which is the cheapest moment to change your mind.

How much does UI/UX design cost?

A focused design project starts under ₹50,000. Full product design depends on the number of screens and the research involved, and is quoted after a short discovery call.
