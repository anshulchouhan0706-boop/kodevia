# Kodevia: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner asked for a
premium website for their web and app development company, pointed at the brand
boards in `kodevia themes/`, and said "Go ahead and design. Be like a
professional... so do it." No interview was run. Everything below that is not a
quoted fact from the brand boards is an authored decision, marked as such.

## Evidence (from the brand boards)

- Name: **kodevia**. Descriptor: **Web & App Development**.
- Mark: a 3D "K" built from three soft, glossy capsules (a vertical stem, an
  upper arm, a lower leg that crosses in front). Lavender-lit, on near-black,
  with a purple floor glow.
- Wordmark: rounded heavy lowercase, the dot of the "i" set in brand purple.
- Palette: Primary Purple `#8B5CF6`, Light Purple `#C084FC`, Off White
  `#F5F5F7`. Neutrals `#0A0A0B` background, `#1A1A1F` surface, `#2E2E36`
  border, `#A1A1AF` muted text. Usage ratio 60 / 25 / 10 / 5.
- Gradients: primary `#8B5CF6 → #C084FC`, dark `#1A1A1F → #8B5CF6`.
- Patterns: large soft capsules and rounded blobs, matte noise texture,
  gradient flow.
- Copy on the boards: "Ideas into Digital Experiences", "Web & App Development
  that people love to use", "Build Digital Experiences", CTAs "Get Started" and
  "Start a Project".

Not supplied, therefore not invented: client names, case studies, testimonials,
team, numbers, pricing, location, email address. The page carries **no counters
and no stats**. The contact address is a single constant in the page and must be
replaced with the real one before launch.

## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** soft-solid, precise, quietly confident. References: the brand's own
   3D mark under a single studio light; a product launch keynote where the
   object is assembled on stage; the feeling of a well-made physical toy
   clicking together.
2. **Journey:** the idea (scattered pieces) → why projects usually go wrong
   (pieces from different places) → the pieces come together as one thing →
   what we can build → how a project runs → start your project.
3. **Energy:** calm open, quieter second beat, the loudest moment in the
   middle, then steady and practical, then a calm resolved close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the curve. The moment: the logo builds
   itself out of the three things the studio does.
5. **What no other site does:** the brand mark is not a logo on the page; it is
   the page's story. You assemble it with your scroll, one discipline at a time.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, because the brand boards are already that
   (near-black, one hue family, generous space). Earned by the brand, not
   defaulted to.
7. **One world or distinct scenes:** distinct scenes, carried by one persistent
   3D object that appears at the open, the peak and the close.
8. **Assets:** three brand boards (low-res composites with labels, not usable as
   page imagery). No footage, no photography, no kie.ai key. So the mark is
   **rebuilt as a real 3D model** in WebGL and every still on the page is
   rendered from that same scene: one light, one material, one shoot.

## Visitor and promise

- **Who:** founders and teams who need a website, a web app or a mobile app.
- **Believe by the end:** design, web and apps from one team fit together.
- **One action, one label everywhere:** **Start a project**.

## Journey

```
1  Possibility   an idea floating in pieces
2  Recognition   projects usually arrive in mismatched pieces
3  Turn          the pieces become one thing
4  Range         what that one team can build
5  Substance     how a project actually runs
6  Commitment    start a project, carrying what you picked
```

## Feeling curve

```
1  Curiosity      three glossy capsules hang apart in depth, the cursor nudges them
2  Quiet unease   small honest type on near-black, the pieces drift away and dim
3  Awe → delight  each capsule flies in, labelled, and docks; the K lands on its floor with a pulse of light
4  Appetite       services travel sideways, each one picked up into your brief with one tap
5  Trust          a wipe opens onto a macro of the material, then four plain steps
6  Resolve        the small finished mark, your picks already filled in, one button
```

No two adjacent lines share a feeling.

## The peak

> "I scrolled and the logo built itself out of the three things they do."

Lives in act 3 (Assembly). It gets the largest span on the page (3.6), the
only per-capsule labels, the light pulse, and it is preceded by act 2, the
quietest screen on the page.

**Authored silence:** act 2 is intentionally sparse (small type, dimmed scene).
It is the silence before the peak, not dead scroll.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **you scroll and the company's logo assembles itself out
of design, web and apps, and then you can tug it apart with your cursor and it
snaps back.**

## Grammar

Filmic one-shot. Why the other seven lost:

- **Chaptered editorial** bans media above the fold; the 3D mark is the brand's
  strongest asset and the standing hero preference requires a layered hero.
- **Live surface** needs a real running product; an agency's product is its
  service, and a fake dashboard is banned.
- **Continuous world** needs geography and worldflight video legs; there is no
  footage and no generation key, and an agency has no route to fly.
- **Typographic poster** throws away the 3D mark.
- **Gallery / catalog** needs real objects (case studies); none were supplied
  and inventing them is fabrication.
- **Split stage** would force a two-sided argument the brand does not make.
- **Rhythmic cutlist** is for energy brands; this brand is calm and precise.

The honest pitch is one linear transformation, "ideas into digital
experiences", which is exactly a one-shot's single arc.

## Signature move

**Scroll-docked mark.** The three capsules of the real logo are separate
physical objects for the first third of the page. In the peak act each one is
labelled with the discipline it stands for (Design, Web, Apps) by a leader line
that tracks the object in 3D, and docks into the K as the visitor scrolls.
Once assembled, the pointer can tug the pieces apart; they spring back into
exact lockup. Bespoke page code (`kscene.js`), engine untouched.

## Useful interaction

The services rail lets the visitor add services to a brief. The selection
carries into the close, where the inquiry is prefilled with it and composes an
email the visitor sends themselves (no fake "sent" state).

## Feel check (after build)

Intended: curiosity → quiet unease → awe/delight → appetite → trust → resolve.

Felt on first full scroll: curiosity → (not quiet enough: pieces still bright
and big) → the click of the K landing → browsing → steady → settled.

Changed: the pieces beat now dims the lights to ~40%, fades the scene to 40%
and shrinks the pieces, so the peak arrives from real silence. Assembly labels
were colliding with the capsules and the intro line; leaders now project from
each piece's outer tip (lower end for the leg) and the intro moved to the
lower left. Second pass matched the intended curve. On the contact sheet the
peak is the largest visual change and the longest span (3.6).

## Revision 2 (owner feedback: jittery, no micro-interactions, too few elements, labels overlapping)

- Grammar deviation, deliberate: the owner asked for more elements, so the bar
  now carries jump links (Services, Process, FAQ). Filmic one-shot forbids
  multiple entry points; this build now bends that rule at the owner's request.
- Added: tools strip (ticker), "Why teams pick Kodevia" asymmetric grid, FAQ,
  brief tray, footer links, secondary hero action.
- Micro-interactions: arrow nudge and light sweep on buttons, nav underline,
  card spotlight and image zoom, pick icon morph (plus to check) with pop,
  chip pop, focus glow, FAQ icon and panel motion, tray ping.
- Labels: set in columns beside the mark with drawn leader lines; never over it.
