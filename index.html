How a project runs

Four stages, the same every time, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Discover

    We learn the business, the people who will use it and the constraints, and agree what success looks like.

  2. Design

    Flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype you can test with real users, then the visual design.

  3. Build

    Regular builds on a staging link you can open on your own phone. Working software, not status reports.

  4. Launch and care

    Hosting, store submission and analytics, then ongoing support after day one.

Questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us on your free demo call.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A marketing site usually takes weeks and an app usually takes months. You get a timeline at the end of Discover, before you commit to the build.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a short discovery conversation, based on what you need. The quote lists exactly what is included.

Can you work on an existing site or app?

Yes. We redesign, extend and take over maintenance of existing products, starting with a review of what is already there.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Repositories, design files and app store accounts are set up in your name.

Do you support the product after launch?

Yes. Ongoing care covers updates, fixes, monitoring and new features as your product grows.
