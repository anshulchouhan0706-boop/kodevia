Business website checklist: 12 things every site needs

Twelve things every small business website in India needs to win customers and show up on Google, and how to check your own site in ten minutes.

A website does not need to be big to work. It needs to answer a visitor's questions quickly, make contacting you effortless, and give Google enough to understand what you do and where. Use this checklist on your own site; most of it you can check from your phone in ten minutes.

Win the visitor

  1. Say what you do in the first screen. A visitor should know what you offer, for whom and where, without scrolling. "Welcome to our website" says nothing; "Furniture showroom in Indore, delivery across MP" says everything.
  2. Make it easy to contact you. Tap-to-call and WhatsApp buttons on every page, and a short enquiry form that asks only what you need.
  3. Show proof. Real photos of your work, products or premises, and genuine reviews or testimonials with names.
  4. Be clear about price. Even a starting price or a range helps visitors decide they can afford you before they call.
  5. Look right on a phone. Most of your visitors are on mobile. Text should be readable without zooming and buttons easy to tap.
  6. Load fast. If the page takes more than a few seconds on mobile data, many visitors leave before they see it. Oversized images are the usual culprit.

Help Google find you

  1. One clear page per service, each with its own heading and a description of what is included, rather than everything squeezed onto the home page.
  2. Page titles that match searches, such as "Interior designer in Indore" rather than "Home".
  3. Your name, phone and area on every page, usually in the footer, written exactly as on your Google Business Profile.
  4. HTTPS, the padlock in the address bar. Browsers warn visitors away from sites without it.
  5. Google Search Console set up, with your sitemap submitted, so you can see which searches bring visitors and spot problems early.

Keep it working

  1. Someone looking after it. Updates, backups, security patches and a check that the forms still deliver. A broken enquiry form can cost you leads for weeks before anyone notices.

How did your site do?

If you ticked ten or more, your website is in good shape; focus on content and reviews. If you missed several in the first group, visitors are probably leaving without contacting you. If you missed the second group, Google is likely struggling to show you for the searches that matter.
