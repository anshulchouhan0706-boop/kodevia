How to get your business found on Google: SEO basics for small businesses

The SEO basics that help small businesses appear on Google: Business Profile, local search, page speed, content and technical setup.

A beautiful website only helps if people can find it. Search engine optimisation (SEO) sounds technical, but the basics that matter most for a small business are practical and within reach.

1. Claim your Google Business Profile

For local businesses this is often the single most important step. A complete, verified profile with the right category, hours, photos, phone number and website link helps you show up in Maps and local results. Ask happy customers to leave reviews and reply to them.

2. Make every page about one clear topic

Search engines match pages to what people type. Give each service its own page, with a clear heading and a title that uses the words customers actually search for, such as "website design in Indore" rather than a slogan.

3. Write titles and descriptions for people

The page title and meta description are what appear in search results. Keep the title short and specific and the description helpful. They are your advert in the results page.

4. Be fast and mobile-friendly

Most searches happen on phones. Pages that load quickly and work well on small screens give visitors a better experience, and search engines take page experience into account.

5. Get the technical basics right

  • A sitemap submitted in Google Search Console
  • HTTPS on every page
  • Clean, readable addresses for each page
  • Structured data that describes your business, services and FAQs
  • Descriptive alt text on images

6. Publish genuinely useful content

Answer the questions your customers ask before they buy, in plain language. Helpful articles earn visits, links and trust over time.

Be patient, and measure

SEO builds over weeks and months, not days. Google Search Console shows which searches bring people to your site, so you can see what is working and improve the rest.
