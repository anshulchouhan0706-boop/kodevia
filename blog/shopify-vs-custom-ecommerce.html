Shopify or a custom e-commerce website: which should you choose?

Comparing Shopify and a custom-built online store on cost, control, fees, design and growth, to help you choose the right e-commerce setup.

If you want to sell online, the first big decision is the platform. Hosted platforms like Shopify get you selling quickly. A custom-built store gives you full control. Here is how to weigh them.

Why businesses choose Shopify

  • Speed to launch. Products, payments and checkout work out of the box.
  • Hosting and security handled. The platform looks after servers and core updates.
  • Apps. Reviews, shipping, discounts and marketing tools can be added.

The limits to know about

  • Ongoing costs. A monthly subscription, possible transaction fees and paid apps add up over time.
  • Checkout and data rules. Some parts of the experience can only be customised within the platform's limits.
  • Lookalike stores. Popular themes mean many shops share the same layout unless the design is customised.

Why businesses choose a custom store

  • A distinctive brand experience from home page to checkout.
  • Custom rules such as B2B pricing, made-to-order products, subscriptions or local delivery zones.
  • Direct integrations with your inventory, billing or delivery partners.
  • No platform fees on top of your payment gateway.

The trade-off is a larger initial build and responsibility for hosting, security and maintenance, usually handled by your development partner.

A simple way to decide

  1. Standard catalogue and standard checkout, and you want to start fast: a hosted platform is a sensible start.
  2. Unusual products, pricing or operations, or a brand that must stand out: custom is usually worth it.
  3. Planning a mobile app as well: a custom backend can serve the store and the app together.
