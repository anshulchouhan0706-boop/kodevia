How to get your Indore business on Google Maps

A step-by-step guide to creating and verifying a Google Business Profile, so customers in Indore find you on Maps and in local search.

When someone in Indore searches "bakery near me" or "CA in Vijay Nagar", Google usually shows a map with three businesses before any website. Those places come from Google Business Profile, a free listing every local business can claim. If you are not on it, you are missing the customers who are ready to visit or call today.

Step 1: Check whether you already have a listing

Search your business name and area on Google Maps. Google sometimes creates a listing automatically from public information. If one exists, use Claim this business on it rather than creating a duplicate; two listings for one business confuse customers and split your reviews.

Step 2: Create or claim your profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account you want to manage the business from. Use a business account, not a personal one you might lose access to.
  2. Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your signboard and invoices. Do not add keywords like "best" or the area name unless they are part of your real name.
  3. Choose the most specific primary category you can, such as "Bakery" rather than "Store". You can add secondary categories later.
  4. Decide whether customers visit you. Shops, clinics and offices add their address. Businesses that travel to customers, or work from home, choose a service area instead and hide the address.
  5. Add your phone number and website.

Step 3: Verify it

Google needs to confirm the business is real before the listing appears in full. Depending on your business, it may ask for a short video of your premises and signage, a phone or email code, or a postcard to your address. Follow the option it offers; verification can take from a few minutes to several days.

Step 4: Complete every section

  • Hours, including special hours for festivals and holidays, so nobody arrives to a closed shutter.
  • Photos of your storefront, interior, products and team. Real photos build far more trust than stock images.
  • Services or products with short descriptions and, where it makes sense, prices.
  • A description in plain words: what you do, who you serve and where.
  • Attributes such as wheelchair access, online payments or women-led, where they apply.

Step 5: Ask for reviews, and reply to all of them

Reviews are one of the strongest signals for the map results, and they are what customers read before choosing. Ask happy customers directly, at the moment they are happiest: after a delivery, at the counter, or in a WhatsApp thank-you message with your review link. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly. Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for them; Google removes them and can suspend the listing.

Step 6: Keep it alive

Post updates, offers and new photos every few weeks, answer questions customers ask on the listing, and keep your hours current. An active profile tells Google the business is open and cared for.

Make your website and profile agree

Your business name, phone number and area should read exactly the same on your Google profile, your website, Instagram, JustDial and every directory. Matching details help Google trust that all of them describe one business. Your website should also load fast on a phone and make it easy to call, message or get directions, because that is where many profile visitors go next.
